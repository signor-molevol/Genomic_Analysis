# Grading Rubric for posters (100 Points)


## Introduction	

### Excellent: Includes 2 – 4 bullet points that are concise and are the most important information needed to convince the reader that this was an important study that needed to be conducted. If bullet points refer to results found in other sources, proper references are included.
20 pts	

### Proficient

Includes information that relates to the purpose of the study, but does not clearly convince the reader of the need for the study
10-15 pts	

### Partially proficient
Includes background information related to the study, but some of the information does not pertain to why the study was conducted.
0-7 pts

## Scientific Question	

### Proficient
The main question or questions being addressed in the study is/are clearly stated in this section.
10 pts	

### Proficient
The main question being addressed in the study is stated but the clarity of the question could be improved.
7 pts

### Partially proficient
A scientific question is stated, but it is not the main question of the study.
0 -5 pts


## Methods

### Excellent
A Box and arrow flow chart used to depict methods for the study.

All major methods are shown in this section.  (See Methods grading rubric for a list of possible methods that you used).

Pictures are used to illustrate the methods, if they can make the methods more easy to understand.

The connections between methods are clearly shown
20 pts	

### Proficient
A Box and arrow flow chart used to depict methods for the study, and all methods mentioned under “Excellent” are described,
but could have been more clearly or accurately presented.
15 pts	

### Partially proficient
All methods mentioned under “Excellent” are NOT shown in this section.
0 – 10 points

## Results	

### Excellent
All results that are needed to determine if you do or do not have support for your hypothesis are shown in this section. 
Whenever possible, the results should be shown in graphical form, with a legend that describes the purpose of the graph.  
This section should include little or no text, except the figure legends.  
All axis titles and text on the graphs should be at least 24 pt. font.
20 pts	

### Proficient
The results described under “Excellent” are included but:
Are not presented clearly, or
do not contain a clear or informative legend, or
contain text below the minimum font size.
15 pts	

### Partially proficient
Some, but not all of the results needed to show support or no support for your hypothesis are presented. 
0 – 10 pts


## Conclusions	

### Excellent
Includes statement about whether or not the hypotheses for the study were supported and 
Includes clear statements as to why the hypothesis was or was not supported.
Provides insightful conclusions that demonstrate that the authors understand the implications of the results.
Includes thoughts on future experiments that could be conducted, if appropriate.
10 pts	

### Proficient
Includes statement about whether or not the hypotheses for the study were supported and includes clear statements as to why the hypotheses was or was not supported.

Provides adequate conclusions that demonstrate that the authors understand the implications of the results, but not to the level required for an “excellent” understanding
7 pts	

### Partially Proficient
Provides only cursory conclusions that DO NOT FULLY demonstrate that the authors understand the implications of the results.
0 – 5 pts

## Visual	

### Excellent
Poster is visually appealing and allows the viewer to be able to quickly determine the purpose and main conclusions of the study.  
Text, pictures and figures are not crowded.  Adequate amount of “white space” is still present on the poster.

Font size of text and type of text on poster is appropriate (no fonts less than 24 point, titles can be distinguished from text, font is used appropriately to draw attention to important points on the poster)

Pictures provide scientific information pertinent to the study and are not used just as decoration. 
20 points	Poster only achieves 2 of the criteria listed under “excellent”

15 points	Poster only achieves <2 of the criteria listed under “excellent”

0 – 10 points
