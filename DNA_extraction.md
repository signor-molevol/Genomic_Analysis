# DNA extraction

At this stage the flies have already been ground up and incubated with proteinase K overnight. This helps lyse the cells and nuclear membranes to release the DNA.


## Centrifuge for three minutes


Remove liquid without removing the fly pieces at the bottom of the tube. 

Put the liquid in a new tube.


## Add 4 ul of RNAse A to the sample. 

Mix by flicking the tube and leave for two minutes.

## Add 150 ul buffer AL

## Add 280 ul Buffer MB

## Add 40 ul of Magnetic beads

Mix and incubate for three minutes.

## Put tube on magnet and wait for the liquid to clear.

Remove the liquid.

## Add 700 ul Buffer MW1 and take the tube off the magnet. 

Mix and incubate for two minutes.

Put the tube on the magnet and remove the liquid.


## Add 700 ul Buffer MW1 and take the tube off the manget

Mix and incubate for two minutes.

Put the tube on the magnet and remove the liquid.

## Add 700 Buffer PE to the sample

Mix and incubate for two minutes.

Put the tube on the magnet and remove the liquid.

## Add 700 Buffer PE to the sample

Mix and incubate for two minutes.

Put the tube on the magnet and remove the liquid.

## Make sure all the liquid is removed. 

If you need to, spin down the tube again. Use a smaller pipette to get the last of the liquid.

## Add 50 ul of H20 to the sample. 

Take the sample of the magnet and resuspend the beads. 

Mix at room temperature for three minutes

## Put tube on the magnet

## THE DNA IS NOW IN THE WATER

## Pipette up the water and put it in a new tube. Label it and place it on ice in the front of the classroom.
